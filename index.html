Palette: Abstract synths

On Auto, the camera reads the scene (light, color, warmth, texture, movement) and picks the band. It changes palettes only at the end of a musical phrase.

    Pixels
    Each object's own pixels write its little melody, and in Abstract synths they shape its tone too.
    Granular
    The biggest thing plays as a granular cloud: its instrument chopped into tiny slices and smeared into a shimmer. Very textured objects get the same treatment.
    Energy
    Movement and busy detail raise the energy: more drums, more notes, faster tempo within the palette's range. Calm scenes thin out and slow down.
    Size
    The biggest thing becomes a slow drone (always a sustained instrument). Large objects play long low notes; small ones sparkle up high and swap to a struck or plucked version of their instrument.
    Height
    Higher in the frame plays higher notes.
    Texture
    Smooth surfaces sound pure. Busy textures add grit, brighter tone and more rhythmic hits.
    Shape
    Round shapes swell in softly. Jagged, thin shapes hit fast.
    Position
    Left and right in the frame pans the sound left and right.
    Movement
    Moving objects wobble with vibrato.
    The whole scene
    Its overall color sets the key and its brightness sets the mood. A slow chord progression keeps every voice in harmony.
