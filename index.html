How objects become sound

Pixels
Each object's own pixels are turned into its sound wave and its little melody, so the same mug always sounds like itself.
Color
Warm colors are reedy, greens are plucky, blues are glassy, purples ring like bells, greys are breathy.
Size
The biggest thing becomes a slow drone. Large objects play long low notes, small ones sparkle up high and fast.
Height
Higher in the frame plays higher notes.
Texture
Smooth surfaces are pure tones. Busy textures add grain and more rhythmic hits.
Shape
Round shapes swell in softly. Jagged, thin shapes pluck.
Position
Left and right in the frame pans the sound left and right.
Movement
Moving objects wobble with vibrato.
The whole scene
Its overall color sets the key and its brightness sets the mood. A slow chord progression keeps every voice in harmony.
